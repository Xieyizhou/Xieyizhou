## How I Work

I use **AI-assisted development and research** as a force multiplier, but I try to keep the important parts explicit:

* source validation over unsupported claims
* reproducible experiments over one-off results
* tests and evaluation over intuition alone
* clear boundaries between observed data, modeled assumptions, and inference
* human judgment for final decisions

AI helps me move faster; **evidence determines what I keep**.
